The Reset Room

Pressure builds in teams - this is the release valve.

In most workplaces

Your people are carrying more than you can see.

Fast-growing companies are extraordinary but demanding places to work. The pressure to perform doesn't pause for the rest of life. And most of the time, there's nowhere inside the organisation to put it down.

Someone is managing a parent's terminal illness between meetings, and saying nothing about it.
A brilliant team member is quietly falling apart through a divorce. Their performance is slipping and no-one knows why.
A leader is carrying the team's anxiety on top of their own, with nowhere to off-load.
Someone is technically exceptional but creating friction in the team. It's going unaddressed.
The cost is not always visible - but it pervades everything.

Unaddressed human challenges show up as dipping performance, rising tension, and quiet disengagement. The organisation you've worked hard to build absorbs all of it, whether you name it or not.

The solution

The Reset Room

You designate a space

A private room in your work environment becomes the Reset Room for the day. No setup required - just a door that closes.

Your people book in

Everyone in the organisation, leaders included, has access to a private 30 to 60 minute session. Completely voluntary.

I hold the space

No reporting. No hidden agendas. Legally bound confidentiality. A place to pause performance mode and reboot.

Not an EAP. Not a wellness day.

  • Traditional EAPs rely on employees seeking help themselves, but most never do.
  • Wellness days offer a surface reset. They don't touch what's underneath.
  • HR support carries reporting obligations. People self-censor.
  • The Reset Room is embedded - it comes to your people, consistently.
  • It works at depth - real conversations, real shifts.
  • Complete confidentiality. No reporting. No agenda beyond the person in the room.
