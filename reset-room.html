The Reset Room

Pressure builds in teams and cripples performance — this is the release valve.

How does it work?

The Reset Room is an easy to implement optimisation strategy for teams under sustained pressure.

You designate a private room in your work environment for me to counsel individual employees.

No reporting. No hidden agendas. Purely a space to pause performance mode and reboot heart and mind.
